About the clinic

Comprehensive dentistry, designed around you.

Dr. Bobby Thomas Dental & Implant Centre brings 25+ years of advanced, comprehensive dentistry to Bangalore, from our clinics in Domlur and Magrath Road. We look beyond individual teeth to understand your complete oral health, restore function, preserve natural teeth and create smiles that feel as good as they look.

We don’t believe in tooth-by-tooth dentistry. A tooth does not function alone. It works with your gums, bone, bite, muscles and jaw joints, and your smile has to belong naturally to your lips, face and overall appearance.
